About

01
NS

I started out hand-coding tables in a text editor and never quite lost that love for clean markup. These days I build layouts with CSS Grid and Flexbox, but the instinct is the same: structure first, style second.

Over the past year I've completed a front-end development track focused on semantic HTML, responsive CSS, and the fundamentals of accessible design. I care about pages that load quickly, read well on any screen, and make sense to someone using a keyboard or a screen reader.

Outside of code, I sketch layout ideas on paper before I open a text editor — it keeps me honest about hierarchy before I get lost in the details.
